How it works

  1. 1

    Share a code

    One person starts a group and gets a 4-letter code. Friends join on their own phones. No accounts.

  2. 2

    Everyone taps in

    Cravings, limits like vegetarian or halal, and a budget from $ to $$$. Screens update live as friends answer.

  3. 3

    Get one pick

    Places that break anyone's limit or budget are dropped. The rest are ranked by how many people want them.
